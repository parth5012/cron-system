SIH26176 Pipeline

How NCMRWF datasets directly feed ORCA's 4 specialized decision agents

sea_checker (Ocean Dynamics) ocean-data

Ingests 3-hourly SST, SSS, SSH, and Mixed-Layer Depth from NEMOVAR (N2OA) to compute upwelling & ocean state.

weather_agent (Atmospheric) hourly-single-levels

Extracts hourly 10m/50m U/V Winds, Wind Gusts, Visibility, and MSLP pressure gradients over coastal sectors.

danger_agent (Severe Weather) mera

Blends 4 km MERA Radar/Satellite Rainfall to trigger storm-risk vetoes and safety warnings for small fishing crafts.

fish_finder (PFZ Validation) imdaa-daily

Correlates thermal fronts and chlorophyll upwelling proxy signals against official INCOIS PFZ polygons.
