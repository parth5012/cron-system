How it works — in plain English (3 steps)

  • 1. You ask — Fisherman says “where can I fish today?” by voice in any language → ORCA hears and understands it.
  • 2. 4 helpers check in parallel — Fish Finder, Sea Checker, Weather & Tide, Danger Watch each check ISRO satellite data.
  • 3. ORCA decides & explains — Smart Combiner picks the best safe fish spot, shows it on map + sends voice/SMS alert with proof.
