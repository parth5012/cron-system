Description

**Sponsoring Body:** Ministry of Home Affairs | **Department:** Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C),CIS Division department; --> **Theme:** Blockchain & Cybersecurity | **Category:** Software | **Priority:** YES - TOP PRIORITY (10/10) **Official URL:** https://sih.gov.in/sih2026PS (search PS ID: SIH26184) --- &#8226; Background The National Cybercrime Reporting Portal is the centralized Portal, which is serving the whole country. Currently, the Portal facilitates citizens in filing complaints, LEAs act on complaints, Banking/Financial Institutions for their actions along with reports/graphs being pulled on daily basis. Presently, the Portal is receiving approximately 8000 complaints on daily basis. The number of complaints has increased manifold during the past months, and this will continue to rise in future. To address the issue of increasing cybercrimes, the proactive approach shall be adopted.<br> &#8226; Description This framework focuses on the mitigation of cybercrimes by adopting a proactive approach. The framework's output will enable the prediction of likely cash withdrawal locations, which, in turn, will allow law enforcement agencies (LEAs)<br><br> at the state and local levels, coordinated by I4C, to implement proactive interventions. These interventions could include deploying special teams or alerting local banks and ATMs in high-risk areas.
