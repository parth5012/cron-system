The National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 emphasizes the importance of vocational education and aims to integrate it into the mainstream education system to prepare students for various career paths. Description: The goal is to transform vocational education into a core component of the elementary and secondary education system, as envisioned in NEP 2020.
