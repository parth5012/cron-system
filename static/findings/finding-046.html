Accurate representation of the vertical ocean temperature is essential for applications such as marine heatwave monitoring, fisheries, and data assimilation, etc. However, direct measurements of subsurface temperature remain sparse because they rely primarily on in-situ observing systems such as ARGO profiling floats, moored buoys, gliders, and ship observations. While these observations provide valuable vertical information, their spatial and temporal coverage is insufficient for generating continuous, basin-scale subsurface fields.In contrast, satellite observations provide continuous, large-scale monitoring of surface ocean conditions at relatively high spatial and temporal resolution.
