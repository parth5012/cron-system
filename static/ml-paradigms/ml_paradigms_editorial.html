PARADIGM 02: STRUCTURAL PIPELINE

Unsupervised Learning: Manifolds & Latent Spaces

Extracting geometric order, topological clusters, and anomalous deviations without teacher labels.

The Manifold Hypothesis

Real-world high-dimensional observations (images, waveforms, molecules) concentrate near low-dimensional sub-manifolds embedded within ambient space.

The Information Bottleneck

Forcing high-dimensional data through variational bottlenecks max I(Z; X) - β I(Z; Noise) discards spurious correlations while preserving core structural factors.

Contrastive Representation Learning

Pulling semantically aligned data views together while pushing discordant samples apart constructs robust latent spaces without manual labels.

PARADIGM 03: DYNAMIC CONTROL PIPELINE

Reinforcement Learning: Policy Synthesis in Dynamic MDPs

The closed-loop interaction between agent policy, environment dynamics, and temporal credit assignment.

The Temporal Credit Assignment Problem

Determining which specific action in an extended 10,000-step trajectory was responsible for a delayed terminal reward requires Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE).

Exploration vs Exploitation Frontier

Balancing epistemic uncertainty exploration (via entropy bonuses, curiosity bonuses) against exploiting known high-value trajectory corridors.

Policy Gradient Clipping Stability

PPO surrogate clipping restricts policy parameter updates within a trust region |r_t(θ) - 1| ≤ ε, preventing policy collapse during training.
