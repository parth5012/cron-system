Major DVR/NVR manufacturers such as Dahua Technology, CP Plus, Honeywell Security, TP-Link, Godrej, Uniview, HIKVISON, and Matrix use proprietary storage formats, file systems, metadata structures, and video encoding mechanisms. During forensic investigations, surveillance footage serves as crucial digital evidence; however, the lack of standardization across DVR/NVR vendors makes acquisition, recovery, analysis, and validation difficult. Investigators often rely on multiple vendor-specific tools, resulting in increased investigation time, inconsistent results, timestamp synchronization issues, challenges in deleted footage recovery, and difficulties in maintaining evidence integrity.
