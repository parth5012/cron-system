**Sponsoring Body:** Ministry of Home Affairs | **Department:** Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C),CIS Division department; --> **Theme:** Blockchain & Cybersecurity | **Category:** Software | **Priority:** YES - MEDIUM-HIGH PRIORITY (8.5/10) **Official URL:** https://sih.gov.in/sih2026PS (search PS ID: SIH26182) --- &#8226; Background The rapid adoption of Virtual Digital Assets (VDAs) and decentralized blockchain ecosystems has significantly increased the complexity of cybercrime investigations globally. Law Enforcement Agencies (LEAs) frequently encounter cryptocurrency wallet addresses linked to cyber frauds, ransomware, investment scams, darknet activities, and laundering of crime proceeds.<br><br> Under the existing investigation workflow, LEAs raise lawful information disclosure requests through the SAHYOG Portal to Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs) such as crypto exchanges, custodial wallet providers, and trading platforms. However, in many cases, the suspect wallet identified during investigations belongs to an unhosted wallet or a wallet for which the associated VASP is unknown. This creates major delays in attribution, freezing of assets, and identification of the beneficial owner.<br><br> Blockchain transactions generally pass through multiple intermediary wallets before reaching centralized exchanges.
