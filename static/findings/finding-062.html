However, in highly polluted urban landscapes like Delhi NCR, there is a critical, dynamic feedback loop between the weather and pollutants. During peak pollution seasons (such as the winter stubble-burning period), atmospheric inversion layers trap particulate matter close to the ground. Conversely, dense concentrations of aerosols (PM2.5) block sunlight,altering local temperatures, wind patterns, and planetary boundary layer (PBL) heights. Ignoring these coupled meteorological-chemical feedback loops leads to significant inaccuracies in standard AQI predictions.
