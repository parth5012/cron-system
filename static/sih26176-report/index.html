Problem Statement

ORCA — Marine EcOsystem Reasoning Collaborative Agents

Agentic AI-powered conversational platform to access, analyze & reason over marine intelligence — PFZ, SST, chlorophyll, weather, disaster alerts, geofencing & routing — on top of ISRO/INCOIS Earth Observation data.

OrganizationIndian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) — Dept. of Space
Theme / CategoryDisaster Management · Software
PS NumberSIH26176
Expected UsersFishermen, researchers, coastal authorities, disaster mgmt, maritime operators
Why this PS exists: India generates vast daily EO oceanographic volumes (SST, chlorophyll, forecasts) via ISRO + global agencies, but stakeholders lack a unified, conversational, explainable layer. ORCA is meant to leapfrog dashboards into a multi-agent, multilingual, geospatial reasoning copilot.

At a Glance · What ORCA Must Do

IntentUnderstand natural language + auto-detect/respond in user's Indian regional language + multi-turn context
AutonomyDecompose complex request → coordinate specialized agents (planning, data discovery, weather, ocean analytics, geo-reasoning, risk, viz, reporting)
DataIntegrate satellite EO + GIS + weather + ocean observations + advisories (public domain: INCOIS/MOSDAC/Copernicus/IMD)
ReasoningSpatial-temporal, correlate heterogeneous sources, explain recommendation with evidence & confidence
SafetyProactive alerts: adverse weather, high waves, lightning, cyclones; geofencing (IMB, restricted waters, MPAs) & route optimization
8 canonical queries (from PS)
  1. Where is nearest Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ) today?
  2. Is it safe to venture to sea tomorrow morning?
  3. What are tide / weather / sea conditions near my location?
  4. Any lightning / cyclone alerts in my area?
  5. Which regions show high chlorophyll + favourable SST?
  6. Safest route for vessel given weather & sea-state?
  7. Why has fish productivity declined in this region?
  8. Which zones to avoid (hazardous / geofencing)?

PS 26176 — Full Description Source: sih.gov.in/sih2026PS · modal 11 rows

Background. Marine ecosystem plays vital role supporting livelihoods, food security, biodiversity, maritime transportation, coastal resilience & blue economy. Every day, vast volumes satellite Earth Observation oceanographic data, including Sea Surface Temperature (SST), chlorophyll concentration, weather forecasts, generated by ISRO & other global agencies.

Marine stakeholders — fishermen, researchers, coastal authorities, disaster management agencies, maritime operators — rely on timely access to oceanographic & meteorological information for operational planning & decision-making. Volume, diversity & complexity of marine data continue to increase — growing need for an intelligent conversational platform that enables users to interact naturally with marine information, ask questions, explore scenarios, receive synthesized, evidence-based recommendations tailored to context.

Advances in Agentic AI, conversational intelligence & geospatial technologies present opportunity to fundamentally transform how marine information is accessed & utilized. By integrating satellite EO data with autonomous agents, intelligent conversational decision-support systems can answer questions such as ‘Where is nearest Potential Fishing Zone today?’, ‘Is it safe to venture to sea tomorrow morning?’, ‘What are tide, weather & sea conditions near fishing location?’ — and provide explainable, context-aware recommendations.

Brief. Develop Agentic AI-powered conversational platform enabling users to access, analyze & reason over marine information using natural language. Platform must autonomously interpret user intent, decompose complex requests into executable tasks, coordinate multiple specialized agents, retrieve relevant marine & geospatial datasets, perform spatial-temporal reasoning, and synthesize actionable recommendations through conversational interface.

Solution must integrate information from multiple sources — satellite EO products, GIS layers, weather services, oceanographic observations, marine advisories available in public domain.

Expected Solution. Participants expected to develop Agentic AI-powered Marine Intelligence Platform leveraging collaborative agents, geospatial technologies & satellite EO data to provide intelligent conversational decision support. Solution must demonstrate core principles of Agentic AI — autonomous planning, reasoning, tool selection, task execution, collaboration among specialized agents, explainable decision-making.

Platform must be capable of:

  • Understanding user intent in natural language; automatically identifying language of query & responding in same language (emphasis on Indian regional languages)
  • Supporting contextual, multi-turn conversations so users can refine queries & explore related scenarios
  • Autonomously discovering, retrieving & integrating relevant satellite, marine, meteorological & geospatial datasets
  • Performing spatial, temporal & contextual reasoning correlating observations from multiple heterogeneous sources
  • Generating explainable, evidence-based recommendations supported by maps, charts, geospatial visualizations & marine advisories
  • Enhancing fishermen safety via proactive alerts (adverse weather, high waves, lightning, cyclones)
  • Geofencing notifications when approaching international maritime boundaries, restricted waters, MPAs, ecologically sensitive zones
  • Assisting route optimization, safe navigation & operational planning based on prevailing & forecast marine conditions

Architecture guidance. Participants encouraged to design modular multi-agent architecture comprising specialized agents for planning, marine data discovery, weather intelligence, ocean analytics, geospatial reasoning, risk assessment, visualization, reporting & user interaction. Architecture must demonstrate autonomous collaboration among agents to solve complex marine intelligence problems while providing intuitive conversational experience.

4 · What Wins SIH26176 — Differentiation Playbook

Don't Rebuild — Orchestrate

  • Consume, don't replicate PFZ. INCOIS generates PFZ daily from OCEANSAT. Wire INCOIS PFZ API + MOSDAC OCEANSAT + IMD warnings + Copernicus backup as tools for your Data Discovery agent. Judges penalize rebuilding PFZ model.
  • Show agent swarm, not chatbot. Demo trace: Orchestrator → [PFZ Agent, Weather Agent, Ocean Agent, Risk Agent, Geo Agent] → Synthesizer for query "safe tomorrow?" with 5 parallel tool calls. One agent = disqualification.
  • Bhashini / Sarvam / AI4Bharat. Auto-detect Marathi/Tamil/Telugu/Malayalam/Kannada + respond same language. FFMA proved 8 langs wins fishermen trust — ORCA must beat it with LLM fluency.
  • Explainability wins. Every answer: citations (INCOIS bulletin ID, MOSDAC granule, IMD warning no.), confidence, map overlay. No black-box.

Architecture That Scores

  • Modular 7–9 agents: Planner · Data Discovery · Weather Intel · Ocean Analytics · Geo Reasoning · Risk Assessment · Viz · Reporting · Interaction. See interactive arch diagram.
  • Geofencing + Nabhmitra/GEMINI hook: Mock or integrate NavIC boundary alert — PS explicitly asks. No competitor has agentic geofencing.
  • Offline-first at sea: Cache PFZ + 48h forecast on device, sync via GEMINI sat link when mobile dead. Differentiate from Windy (online-only).
  • Eval harness: 30-script Hindi/Marathi/Tamil query set with expected multi-source reasoning; measure planning accuracy, tool recall, answer faithfulness.
Pitch line for PPT: "INCOIS tells you where fish are. FFMA tells you in Tamil. ORCA tells you — in your Tamil — why that zone is safe today, which zone to avoid tomorrow, and the safest route there, with maps to prove it."

Verdict — Completion Status

All Indian govt systems completed & operational nationally (INCOIS since 2002, Bhuvan/MOSDAC continuous, GEMINI/Nabhmitra deployed). Private startups pilot/unscaled. No product has completed the ORCA combination — conversational + agentic + multilingual + geospatial reasoning + geofencing + explainability. That's the white-space ISRO is funding. Your SIH prototype should be the first to demo the full intersection, not a better PFZ map.
